Each price is the typical price you'd pay at an ordinary shop or provider in that city — not the lowest or highest. The smaller “usually” range shows how much it varies between shops and seasons. Prices are estimated from cost-of-living data, so treat them as a guide, not a quote. Indian fuel, LPG, vehicle and some grocery prices are set individually; vehicles are approximate on-road prices. Home services are labour only, per visit, for a basic job. Items marked “live” are updated every morning: gold and silver from world spot prices, and Indian vegetables, fruit and grains from government market (mandi) prices, converted to a typical shop price. Before you buy, use the links under each item to check today's price. Conversions use the exchange rates shown above; tap Refresh for the latest.
