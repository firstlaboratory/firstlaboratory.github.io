Offline tips

All phrases are stored on your phone once the app has loaded. Text, transliteration and Show work with no signal.

Play uses your phone's voices. For offline audio, open Settings → Google Text-to-speech → Install voice data, and download each language you need.

For sentences not in the list, download the language in Google Translate for offline typing.
